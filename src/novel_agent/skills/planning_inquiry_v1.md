# Planning inquiry formation

Separate author-supplied, accepted-Plan-derived, Canon-derived, and
planner-proposed statements. Propose bounded goals and alternatives, then ask
only questions whose answers can change the planning decision. Mark human
choices explicitly; never factualize a future candidate to justify retrieval.

When a missing fact affects a plan, preserve it as a bounded unresolved need
with its chapter range, owner, allowed/forbidden assumptions, and source
references. Do not invent a finding ID, close a host issue, or turn an inquiry
answer into Canon. Stable issue identity, host constraints, and closure history
are produced and checked by the host.
